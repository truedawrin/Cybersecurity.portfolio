## 🛡️ Defensive Perspective

This room is entirely about the defensive perspective. Key takeaways include:

- **User Awareness Training:** Educating users is a critical defense against attacks.
- **Asset Management:** Knowing what systems and devices you need to protect is the first step.
- **Patching & Updates:** Keeping systems up-to-date prevents known vulnerabilities from being exploited.
- **Preventative Devices:** Firewalls and Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS) are core components of a proactive defense.
- **Logging & Monitoring:** Proper logging is essential for detecting malicious activity and intrusions.

## 🧠 What I Learned

- **SOC (Security Operations Center):** A team that monitors a network and its systems to detect malicious events.
- **Threat Intelligence:** Gathering information about potential adversaries to enable a threat-informed defense.
- **DFIR (Digital Forensics and Incident Response):** The process of investigating a cyber attack (forensics) and responding to it (incident response). This also includes **Malware Analysis** (static and dynamic).

## 📚 Key Takeaways

- Defensive security (Blue Team) is about protecting systems and responding to threats.
- It is a multi-layered discipline that includes people, processes, and technology.
- Key focus areas are the SOC, Threat Intelligence, and DFIR.
